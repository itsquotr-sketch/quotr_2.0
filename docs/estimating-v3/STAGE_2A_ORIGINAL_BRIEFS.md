## Internal partition

Build one non-loadbearing internal timber partition, 6m long and 2.4m high, with one opening for a standard internal door. Line both sides with standard plasterboard and insulate the wall cavity. Include the door, jamb, hinges and handle. Stop the plasterboard ready for painting, but exclude painting, electrical work and skirting. Ground-floor access.

## Office fitout

Prepare an estimate for a 120m² office fitout in an occupied commercial building. Include 24 linear metres of new partition walls at 2.7m high, lining both sides, acoustic insulation, six internal door sets, 120m² of carpet tiles and 45 linear metres of skirting. Include painting of the new walls and skirting. Existing flooring has already been removed. We need site establishment, protection of common areas, daily cleaning, final cleaning, deliveries and supervision. No structural, electrical, plumbing or fire-system work is included. Access is by goods lift during booked delivery windows. Duration is not yet confirmed.

## Three-room flooring

Renovate the flooring in three rooms. Bedroom 1 is 3.6m × 3.2m and gets carpet. Bedroom 2 is 4.2m × 3.5m and gets carpet. The hallway is 8m × 1.2m and gets vinyl planks. Remove and dispose of the existing carpet in all three areas. Include underlay where appropriate, transition strips and 10% waste on new floor coverings. Exclude skirting replacement and major subfloor repairs.

## Elevated replacement deck

Replace an existing elevated timber deck approximately 5.2m x 3.1m and around 1.2m above ground. Existing hardwood decking and framing are to be removed. Existing piles may be retained if sound after inspection. Install new hardwood decking, new framing where required, fascia/face boards and one step down. No balustrade is requested. Access is narrow down the side of the property and demolition waste will need to be carried approximately 25–30m to the collection point.

## Laminate flooring

Supply and install 30m² of laminate flooring in an empty rectangular room measuring 6m × 5m. The existing floor is bare concrete, clean, dry and ready for installation. Include underlay and 10% waste on the laminate. No skirting, floor preparation, demolition or disposal is required. Normal weekday access at ground level.

## Low deck

Supply and install decking on a rectangular platform measuring 4,800mm × 3,600mm. The finished deck is 450mm above ground. Use 90mm-wide decking boards with a 5mm gap. Include the substructure, fixings and 10% waste on decking boards. Exclude stairs, balustrades, demolition and painting. Ask for any missing specifications that materially affect the estimate.

## Conflicting flooring area

Install vinyl flooring in a room that is 5m × 4m. The total flooring area is 32m². Use the room dimensions and the stated area to prepare the quote. Include 10% material waste.
